It writes the Owner decision record in the shared shape (kind authorize-operation) and a write-ahead receipt of a new append-only kind to the integration store, never the effects store; merges with Do: merge and head_commit_id, the message carrying the trailers AIEH-Decision-Id, AIEH-Decision-Channel, AIEH-Approved-Head, AIEH-Manifest-Digest, AIEH-Statement-Ref and AIEH-Recorder; reads back the ref, the parent, the digest and merged_by; and resolves a lost answer as unknown by reading the ref, never by merging again. The bootstrap channel is the Owner's verbatim reply in the main-session conversation after the challenge is shown, transcribed by the Overseer and logged with the decision id; it proves a declared decision bound to an exact revision, not an authenticated Owner. merged_by aieh-owner is shown as merged under Owner authority by the recorder on decision <id>, never as merged by the Owner.
