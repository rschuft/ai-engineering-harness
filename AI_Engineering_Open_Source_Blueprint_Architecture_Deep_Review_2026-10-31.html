01 / Product promise

An AI engineering organization you can understand and direct

AI Engineering is a local-first, cross-platform software engineering workspace assembled from existing open-source products. An Owner expresses a desired outcome in conversation. The system captures the objective, proposes a design, plans and performs engineering, independently challenges the implementation, and returns evidence of the delivered result.

One Owner experience

assistant-ui presents Owner conversation, outcomes, and decisions. Embedded Agent Canvas components provide native engineering conversation, files, terminal, and activity. The Owner sees one cohesive workspace without rebuilding the engineering UI.

Reuse before build

AI Engineering pins proven open-source products, configures and connects them, and writes small replaceable integration modules only where no adopted component meets the requirement.

The product promise

Within about thirty seconds of opening AI Engineering, the Owner should see what is being accomplished, why, what has genuinely happened, what is blocked, and what decisions require their authority.

Ordinary engineering and installation tasks are carried out by AI. Consequential Owner decisions remain attributable to the Owner. An approval is not a request for the Owner to operate a terminal or coordinate agents.

Design status: This is a proposed reference architecture. The integrated distribution has not been qualified as an end-to-end product. Upstream capabilities described here still require version-pinned integration tests.

Evidence labels throughout: Upstream documented means verified in public implementation/docs; Proposed boundary describes our design, not a tested installation. Version-pinned qualification remains necessary.

Authority is recordedOpenSpec, Backlog.md and Gitea own the accepted facts. No summary, status projection or agent claim can override their versions.
Capabilities are constrainedA configured agent or Skill requests tools; effective permission comes only from the selected runtime, credentials and host-enforced policy.
Results are independently provenImplementation, CI and delivery evidence are checked at exact revisions before the system says an objective was satisfied.
02 / Owner experience

From one request to a verified outcome

Example: “The application hangs during shutdown. Fix it, prove it closes cleanly, and make the corrected version available.”

01

Understand the actual objective

Conversation retains the original statement. AI Engineering captures the desired behavior and acceptance conditions; “maybe it is a worker thread” remains a hypothesis rather than an authoritative requirement.

02

Propose a design

OpenSpec stores a versioned objective, the selected approach, rationale, tradeoffs and source references. The Owner can inspect the design in plain language and visual form, with searchable links to earlier conversations and decisions.

03

Plan the work

Backlog.md records the engineering tasks, dependencies, readiness and acceptance criteria. Each task points to the objective or other documented reason that justified it.

04

Execute and challenge

OpenHands dispatches a coding provider into an appropriately isolated workspace. The provider reproduces the issue and implements the fix. An independent reviewer challenges the change using the same goal and actual evidence.

05

Present a meaningful decision

assistant-ui presents the proposed outcome, review, tests, risks and consequences. An engineering session opens directly in Agent Canvas. Gitea protects consequential Owner authorization.

06

Finish and report

Authorized automation completes the operation and checks its actual result. The Owner sees outcome status and source-backed evidence, including failures or unresolved uncertainty, without manually relaying an approval back to an agent.

Not everything requires a full ceremony. A routine, reversible repair may need little more than a linked task, implementation evidence and tests. Escalation and formal design artifacts are proportional to risk and consequences.
How an ambiguous request becomes an accepted objective

Intake is an agent-driven refinement process, not an automatic promotion of the most recent chat answer into Work. Use an OpenSpec proposal/schema plus a reusable Intent Normalization Skill; avoid creating a bespoke intent service.

ORIGINAL · IMMUTABLE REFERENCE“It hangs on shutdown. Maybe a thread.”

Conversation retains exact wording, author and source message identity.

DISSECT · ATTRIBUTEDGoal ≠ hypothesis ≠ constraint

Goal: exit reliably. Hypothesis: worker thread. Constraint: preserve work. Unknown: reproducibility and recovery expectations.

NORMALIZE · CHALLENGEBehavior and testable boundaries

Define expected behavior, scenarios, excluded scope, measurable acceptance and threats to durable data.

ACCEPT · VERSIONObjective with durable lineage

OpenSpec records the normalized outcome and separately attributed hypotheses, assumptions and applicable policy requirements.

Ask the Owner only when unresolved ambiguity materially changes the promised outcome, authority, safety, cost or tradeoff. An agent may continue investigating uncertain implementation details autonomously. AI-originated work uses the same flow but must name its observed defect, vulnerability, dependency or adopted obligation as its justification.

01Request a feature or repairIntent → design → implementation → review → usable outcome+

Security-sensitive behaviors the integration must address

Native discovery can override the catalog

Delegation is not privilege reduction

OpenHands sub-agents inherit their parent's resolved MCP servers. Different permission tiers must use different sessions/OS credentials/MCP exposures. Provider differences must be tested

Agent Skills are portable in format, not guaranteed identical in loading priority or tool permissions. Validate the supported skill path, agent mapping, MCP exposure and instruction-precedence behavior separately for each pinned OpenHands/ACP provider combination.

Control verdict versus authorization decision
Assessment verdict

Computed by a trusted assessor from evidence and applicability. Answers whether the control's condition was demonstrated. A stale test cannot satisfy a changed commit or modified policy; lack of a result is not a pass.
