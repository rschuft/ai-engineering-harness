**2. The delta experiment (one run).** Dulwich 1.2.21 exposes deltas publicly in
`dulwich.pack.generate_unpacked_objects(..., deltify=True)` (the writer `Repo.generate_pack_data`
uses passes no `deltify`). The experiment pushed this checkout's `main` (273 commits) through the
real `exchange_push` with `Repo.generate_pack_data` replaced by the same object selection with
`deltify=True`, interleaved with the present writer:
- the present writer: 2.7 s, 28,492,627 bytes, `git bundle verify` rc 0, the round trip through
  `exchange_pull` `verified: true`;
- `deltify=True`: **not finished after 600 s** (stopped; no bundle written), against 2.7 s now.

Decision: **the present writer is kept**; deltify fails the "not slower than now" condition by more
than two orders of magnitude. **Size against git:** git's bundle of the same branch is 9.26 to 9.37
MB, so the module's is **3.1 times larger**, and a repository reaches the 256 MiB
`exchange.push.max_bytes` bound **about 3 times sooner** (about 83 MiB of git-bundle size) than
with git. The bound is unchanged.

on four start/ceiling pairs).
