## The engineering manager's rulings

- **R1** The canonical document is exactly `format` `aieh-provider-envelope/2`, `provider`, `origin`, `expires_at`,
  `selection`; the block refuses `limit` and any of `amount`, `currency`, `enforced_by`, `cap`
  (`CONFIG_ENVELOPE_INVALID`, never ignored); `inference_broker.py envelope` prints the `/2` manifest; a receipt over a
  correctly formed `/1` document authorizes no start.
- **R2** The cap, its ledger writes and reads (`inference/envelope/...`), `ENVELOPE_CAP_REACHED`,
  `ENVELOPE_USAGE_UNKNOWN` and the start order's ledger step are removed; no store data is deleted or rewritten.
- **R3** `BROKER_STOPPING` is kept as part of the clean stop, reworded (no envelope run or usage).
- **R4** A grant is bound to conversation, attempt, API family, model list and lifetime, with no budget; a `budget`
  key is refused with the new stable code `BUDGET_RETIRED`; usage is information only.
- **R5** `inference_control.py issue` and every caller carry the lifetime only.
- **R6** The D1 grant declaration is lifetime only; a declaration still carrying `requests` or `tokens` is refused
  with the module's declaration code.
- **R7** Texts naming the budget or the cap are corrected, changing no other control.
- **R8** Tests asserting a retired budget or cap are rewritten to assert its absence or removed, through the
  documented test-id process.
- **Format versions** (after the review's NIT 1, kept): bumped where a record's shape changed:
  `aieh-provider-envelope/2`, the broker's grant receipt and issue answer `aieh-inference-grant/2`, the D1 grant record
  `ai-engineering-harness/dispatch/inference-grant/v2`. Not bumped: `aieh-inference-broker-start/1`,
  `aieh-inference-broker-status/1` and the revocation answer: only their optional members lost fields, and no
  real-provider record exists.
