## The independent review (a fresh engineer, three passes)

1. **Pass 1:** B1 (blocking) a key repeated in a body read as one document by the broker and another upstream; S1 the
   request headers a deny list; S2 the content checks deny lists; S3 a flight cut during its connect still sent its
   request; S4 the signal handler could deadlock against the main thread's wait (shown at a 0 s slice); S5 the
   stop-race guard untested; NITs, among them N1 (the cut flight's claim), N2 (a pass-through blocked on the upstream
   on Windows), N4 (case-folded reach keys) and N6 (non-ASCII digits in `Content-Length`). 26 mutations, 19 killed.
   **Disposition:** all fixed (`6163b53`, `87f4b9c`, `5469f19`, `d7682b6`), each with a test that fails without it.
2. **Pass 2 (re-review):** pass 1 closed, no new BLOCKING or SHOULD-FIX; 4 NITs (the whole-body source walk refused
   ordinary tool schemas; NUL in header values; non-UTF-8 bodies; a stale TLS handle). **Disposition:** the first three
   fixed in `73c5831` (sources by position, every control character, strict UTF-8); the fourth accepted (below).
3. **Pass 3 (the delta `d7682b6..61e1906`):** NITs 1 to 3 and the mutation-R16 hang (`61e1906`) closed; one
   SHOULD-FIX: position-only source checks admitted five malformed shapes (case-variant `Messages`/`System`, `messages`
   as an object, a message as a list, a url image under an unknown key). **Disposition:** fixed in `008b7d6` (the
   engineering manager's ruling (a) and (b): the top-level-key, messages-shape and system-shape rules; the whole-body
   source walk restored, skipping exactly `tools[].input_schema` and a `tool_use`'s `input`).
4. **The engineering manager's verification at `008b7d6`:** the reviewer's probe (`probe_admit3.py`), 26 cases, each as
   expected: the opaque cases and HTAB admitted; every reach, shape, header-control-character and encoding case
   refused, with nothing forwarded; a lone-surrogate JSON escape in valid UTF-8 admitted (ruling: valid JSON, no
   reach).

## Accepted residuals (the engineering manager's)

- Mutants M02 and M03 (the relay's cut checks) survive: masked by the claim-then-shutdown drain.
- Closing the console window (`CTRL_CLOSE`) does not assure a clean stop: the broker is stopped by Ctrl-C, Ctrl-Break or
  the control stop (a clean stop on a console close would need a native blocking console handler, not built).
- A stale TLS handle window after the detach, confined to flights being cut.
- The provider's response headers (organization and workspace ids: identifiers, not credentials) are relayed to the
  guest; disclosed in the OA-12 package (section 3, assumption 5).
