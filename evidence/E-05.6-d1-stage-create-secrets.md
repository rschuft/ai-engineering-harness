## What was built

- **One shared check** (`integrations/dispatch/create_secrets.py`, `check_secrets`), called by S-03.3's
  qualification admission (`acp_qualification.check_create`) and by the dispatcher's D1 stage
  (`dispatch_inputs.check_create_body` with the stage's secrets). One module under one name; each caller passes its
  own refusal type; the codes and meanings are `create_secrets.MEANINGS`. It refuses: other, additional or missing
  names (`SECRET_NAMES`); any kind but a `StaticSecret` of exactly kind and value (`SECRET_KIND_FORBIDDEN`);
  `secrets_encrypted` not false (`SECRETS_ENCRYPTED`); a provider-key shape anywhere (`SECRET_PROVIDER_KEY_SHAPED`); a
  token value not of the broker's shape (`SECRET_NOT_ATTEMPT_TOKEN`); a token not bound to the reserved conversation
  and attempt through the grant the dispatcher holds (`SECRET_TOKEN_NOT_BOUND`, the dispatcher only); the token, its
  body or any run of 8 or more of its body's characters anywhere but its own secret (`TOKEN_OUTSIDE_SECRET`); any
  destination but the profile's guest inference endpoint (`DESTINATION_NOT_ENDPOINT`). Its refusal details redact
  every word that holds the token, such a part of it, or a provider key's shape.
- **The D1 stage's admission and create** (`integrations/dispatch/dispatch_inputs.py`). The pinned stage template
  carries no secrets; `admit` admits every other input; the token is then requested from the broker's control
  listener (`InferenceAccess`, its revoker checked with the broker's grammar); `admit_secrets` adds the block with
  the token's value as the slot `{attempt_token}`; the checks before the transport put the grant's token in the
  slot's place in memory only, run the shared check with the binding, and `send_create` sends that exact body. The
  names come from the pinned registry extract, the endpoint from the approved profile, the family from
  `acp_qualification.ACP_FAMILY`, the model from the pinned template's ACP block, the budgets and lifetime from
  `provisioning.agent_server.stage_create_template.inference_grant {requests, tokens, ttl_s}` (required, no default).
  Every other stage still refuses `secrets` (`BODY_FIELD_UNKNOWN`).
- **Issue and revocation through the control listener.** The issue is journaled before it is sent
  (`dispatch/attempt/<attempt id>/inference-grant`); after the issue the grant is revoked on any refusal, failure or
  `BaseException`, journaled as `dispatch/attempt/<attempt id>/inference-revocation/<n>`; a lost or invalid answer is
  revoked by conversation and attempt; `end_attempt` ends the grant of an attempt in any state; `send_create` refuses
  `GRANT_REVOKED` before each transport call once a revocation of the attempt's grant is recorded. The broker revokes
  by fingerprint or by conversation and attempt, and tombstones an attempt revoked before its grant registers (a later
  issue is `GRANT_ATTEMPT_REVOKED`), under a per-attempt lock.
- **Custody.** Records, refusals, exceptions and logs carry the token's fingerprint only; a refusal of a body that
  held the token is rebuilt without it and without an exception chain; `AdmittedCreate.to_json()` carries the slot;
  the live transport sends a body that carries secrets sealed (standard input, never a command line).

## The independent review

Three passes by a fresh engineer, each with probes kept in the session's scratch directory.

1. **First pass, findings 1 to 8, all fixed** (`d5fcbd7`, `4966416`): (1) the live transport put the create body on
   a command line, where a `TimeoutExpired` would show it, now sealed; (2 to 4) a lost or invalid issue answer, a
   `KeyboardInterrupt` after the issue and a failed revocation on a failure path left a live grant with no retry, now
   the grant journal, revocation by attempt, revocation on any `BaseException` and `end_attempt` for any state; (5) a
   token value in the admitted body was sent, now `SECRET_NOT_FROM_GRANT`; (6) a token split by dots was recoverable
   from a refusal, now withheld whole; (7) the scrubbed refusal chained to the original, now no chain; (8) the
   revoker was not checked, now the broker's grammar, and unsettled receipts are settled whatever their revoker.
2. **Second pass:** A (a grant registered after a revocation by attempt found none), fixed by the broker's tombstone
   (`8d47cac`, `b977105`); B (an invalid answer's fingerprint of another attempt could be revoked), fixed: own
   attempt only, the attempt's selector decides; C (an attempt ended in flight still sent its create), fixed:
   `GRANT_REVOKED` before each transport call; D, no change (existing behaviour, already asserted).
3. **Third pass, no blocking or should-fix finding:** N1 (one broker lock for every attempt), fixed by a per-attempt
   lock, and N5 (the broker's docstring), fixed (`44a83ab`). Accepted residuals: N2, a same-account caller can
   tombstone an attempt under a wrong conversation (the control listener's same-account reach is disclosed under
   decision 0013 point 6, and a tombstone only refuses a later issue); N3, a broker defect that answers "no grant"
   for a grant it holds would be recorded as no grant; N4, the window between the last `GRANT_REVOKED` check and the
   create's POST fails closed (the revoked token is refused at the broker).
