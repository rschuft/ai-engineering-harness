# P-04.3: dispatch-input decisions (design acceptance, manifest promotion) and load_manifest's enforcement

- **Date:** 2026-11-13.
- **Plan leaf:** P-04.3, "Promote approved dispatch inputs through recorded decisions".
  **Action (verbatim):** "Write the live approved-input manifest (ai-engineering-harness/dispatch/approved-inputs/v1)
  and its entries (repositories, accepted designs, profiles with their sha256, catalog pins and bindings) only
  through a recorded Owner decision bound to the resulting manifest digest, in the shared Owner decision-record
  shape of P-04.1 and P-04.2. Derive each accepted-design entry from a recorded design-acceptance decision, a
  decision kind of its own in that shape. Make load_manifest detect and refuse, each with a stable code, an entry
  written without a recorded decision, a manifest whose digest differs from the decided one, a design entry without
  a design-acceptance record, and a replayed decision."
- **Proof (verbatim):** "An entry written without a recorded decision, or with a digest that differs from the
  decided one, is detected and refused by load_manifest; a design entry without a design-acceptance record is
  refused; a replayed decision is refused; a manifest promoted through a recorded decision bound to its digest
  loads, and each of its entries names its approving record."
- **Authority read:** the plan leaf; decisions 0008 (points 2, 5), 0009, 0010 (point 1), 0012 (points 4 to 6, 9),
  0014 and 0018 (points 3.2, 3.3, 4.3, 4.8, 5, 7); the architect's work design for the leaf (gates B and G) with the
  engineering manager's rulings R1 to R8, F1 to F8 and F11, N-A to N-D. The design as built is decision 0020
  (Proposed), `decisions/0020-dispatch-input-decisions-design-acceptance-manifest-promotion-and-load-enforcement.md`.
- **Repository range:** `54c9920` to the commit that adds this record. Engineer commits: `dca60ac`, `3b752c9` (slice 1);
  `518a026`, `491aa8d` (slice 2); `6878d1b`, `e13607f` (slice 2b, the review's fix-forward); `535183b` (slice 3, the
  re-review's fix-forward); and the commit that adds this record and decision 0020. (`98879d5` in the range is the
