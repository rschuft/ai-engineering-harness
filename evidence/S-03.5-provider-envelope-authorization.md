# S-03.5: the provider envelope, the broker's start precondition, the model selection and the broker-enforced cap

- **Date:** 2026-11-13.
- **Plan leaf:** S-03.5, "Bind the broker's real-provider start to a recorded provider-envelope authorization".
  **Action (verbatim):** "Add to the S-03.2 broker a fail-closed start precondition: before it opens a listener or
  reads the provider key for any family whose upstream is not loopback, serve requires a confirmed, unexpired
  authorization recorder receipt (decision 0012) for the provider envelope and an admitted model selection bound to
  it, and it refuses requests for any model outside that selection. Offline only, against fixtures: no real key,
  provider or egress."
- **Proof (verbatim):** "Offline, against fixtures: started with any family whose upstream is not loopback, serve
  refuses before it opens a listener or reads the provider key, unless the integration store holds a confirmed,
  unexpired authorization recorder receipt (decision 0012) whose operation is the provider envelope and whose target
  is the canonical digest of the live envelope (the provider id, the one upstream origin, the spending limit and the
  expiry). A missing, expired, replayed or mismatched receipt (another origin, limit or digest) is refused with a
  stable code. The model selection is either part of the envelope digest the Owner authorizes, or a delegated
  recorder record under decision 0018 point 3 (P-04.3's delegated decision kind) naming the envelope's receipt and
  decision 0018; a selection with neither authority, missing, or naming another receipt is refused at start with a
  stable code, and a request for a model outside the selection is refused. If the Owner-authorized spending limit is
  not enforced by the provider on the dedicated key, as stated in OA-12's presentation, the envelope carries a
  cumulative cap that the broker enforces and that fails closed when reached or when usage is unknown. Loopback fake
  upstreams (the S-03.2 qualification) start as today."
- **Authority read:** the plan leaf; decisions 0009, 0010 (point 1), 0012, 0015 (as amended; section 6.3), 0018
  (points 3, 4.1, 4.2, 7) and 0020; the architect's work design for the leaf (gates C and G) with the engineering
  manager's rulings R1 to R11 (R6 qualified), the slice rulings and the fix-forward rulings F1 to F7. The design as
  built is decision 0021 (Proposed),
  `decisions/0021-provider-envelope-model-selection-and-broker-enforced-cap.md`.
- **Repository range:** `b8cd6ad` to the commit that adds the inventory and policy records. Engineer commits:
  `6d0b9b2` (slice 1: the envelope and the start precondition); `fb1e656` and `b5928cf` (slice 2: the model
  selection's act, then its checks at start and per request); `5fdc43f` (slice 3: the cumulative cap); `01c970d`
  (slice 3b, the review's fix-forward); the commit that adds this record and decision 0021; and the commit that adds
  the inventory and policy records.
- **Verdict (the engineer's report; the verdict is the engineering manager's):** built and tested offline, against
  fixtures (the real authorization recorder over a fake Gitea called in memory, a throwaway integration store,
  in-process fake upstreams and in-memory upstream connections, synthetic keys and statements); every proof row

## 4. Independent review and re-review

A fresh engineer reviewed slices 1 to 3 at `5fdc43f` (read-only, with scratch reproductions and a mutation run) and
re-reviewed the fix-forward at `01c970d`. Each finding's disposition, as ruled by the engineering manager:

| Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|
| F1 (blocking) the cap failed open after the close receipt: a handler still alive after the broker's stop (a keep-alive connection) could forward a request whose usage no receipt would hold | fixed (`01c970d`): the stop sets a stopping state under the broker's lock before it reads the close's counts; a request of a metered family is then refused `BROKER_STOPPING` (503) before anything is written upstream; tests in process and over a real loopback keep-alive connection |
| F2 the in-memory connection kept its socket after a failed `getresponse`, unlike http.client, so the "written" flag was untested (mutant B19) | fixed (`01c970d`): the fake clears its socket as http.client does; a test over a real loopback socket |
| F3 surviving mutants B3, B14, G3 and E11 | fixed (`01c970d`): a test kills each; `verify_selection` restores the field order of a head of another kind and reads its basis defensively, so its kind refuses it (`MODEL_SELECTION_NOT_AUTHORIZED`) and nothing raises |
| F4 redundant check pairs (each side fails closed alone) | no change, as ruled |
| F5 the TLS-after-connect case | documented: observed on CPython 3.12 on Windows; one transient TLS or certificate failure closes a broker-enforced envelope until a new authorization (carried by the engineering manager to OA-12's presentation) |
| F6 the selection read side reads the last confirmed head only | documented, with why an unsettled selection is harmless |
| F7 the per-request model check reads the body's top-level `model` | documented for the three declared families (carried by the engineering manager to S-03.3) |

The re-review closed F1 to F3 and F5 to F7 with no new finding (F4: no change).
