## 2. The contract

```
resolve -> reserve_attempt -> admit(resolved, manifest=, workspaces=, read_profile=, read_artifact=) -> AdmittedCreate
                           -> send_create(admitted, resolved=, workspaces=, transport=)
                              all checks again, then transport(method, path, body) exactly once
```

- **No caller value.** `admit` and `send_create` take no path, mount, cap, runtime,
  working directory, route or body field (`test_no_parameter_takes_a_caller_choice`).
- **Route:** `POST /api/conversations`, and the profile's pinned route allowlist must hold
  exactly one entry for it with `dispatcher` `allowed`. Anything else is
  `ROUTE_NOT_ALLOWLISTED`.
- **Body:** the pinned create template with only `conversation_id` replaced by the reserved one
  (`conversation_id_for(operation_key)`). Checked: a field allowlist (the template's seven
  fields; in `container`, `browser` and the two caps); the reserved id; `conversation_runtime`
  exactly `docker`, never omitted (decision 0001 s2); `workspace` exactly
  `{"kind": "LocalWorkspace", "working_dir": "/workspace"}`; `container.memory` and `.cpus` only
  as the declared values; every other field equal to the template's. A field outside the
  allowlist is always refused, and its name only picks the code.
- **Policy (policy-inputs/v2 `admission`, read from the approved profile at resolution):**
  server runtime `docker` and not selectable; `max_concurrent_runs` times each declared cap
  within the guest ceilings (`memory_mib`, `vcpu`); the declared binds strictly under
  `source_root`, clear of every forbidden source (a list that must name the Docker and
  containerd sockets), distinct destinations, one of them `/workspace`; the pinned template
  and allowlist paths under `profiles/<id>/` with their sha256. `policy_digest` covers it, so
  the reserved attempt records the ceilings it was admitted under.
- **Host workspace:** `planned_workspace` under the `workspaces` category of the
  `aieh_paths.Resolution` passed in. Refused, in order: a Windows device name in any part, with
  or without an extension (`WORKSPACE_RESERVED_NAME`); anything but
  `<repository>/<WORK-ID>/<attempt uuid>` in the fixed rule's exact grammar
  (`WORKSPACE_PATH_INVALID`); another attempt's path (`WORKSPACE_NOT_PLANNED`); no usable
  category (`WORKSPACE_CATEGORY_UNAVAILABLE`); from the category down, an existing link,
  junction, other reparse point or non-directory (`WORKSPACE_PATH_UNSAFE`). Reads only; creates
  nothing.
- **Mounts:** `AdmittedCreate.mounts` is the effective mount set the container must have: the
  declared binds with the conversation's hex id. It is compared exactly at admission and again
  before the send (`MOUNTS_NOT_DECLARED`). The create body has no mount field; the only body
  routes to a mount (`working_dir`, a container option) are refused.
- **Provenance at `admit`:** the manifest that approved the resolution, the profile bytes
  (`PROFILE_DRIFTED`), the policy digest and the profile's own admission policy
  (`ADMISSION_MISMATCH`), and the pinned artifacts (`PROFILE_ARTIFACT_*`).
- **Defence in depth at `send_create`:** the admission policy is re-derived from the reserved
  record's digest-bound policy inputs and its rules re-checked; then every check runs again on
  the `AdmittedCreate` (identity and digest, workspace with the on-disk walk, the template's
  sha256, route, body, mounts). The body sent is the one just validated. A transport failure is
  raised; nothing is retried or re-routed.

## 7. Findings

Classified by the fix-forward rule.

- **F1 (introduced-by this slice; repaired before commit).** Two forged-policy tests could not
  tell the policy-side `working_dir` and create-route rules from the request-side compares
  (round 1 above). Repaired in the tests; round 2 caught all 46.
- **F2 (introduced-by this slice; repaired before commit).** The first run of the new tests had
  two harness errors: `None` doubled as "use the default resolution" (a test meant to pass
  `None` got the default), and a pinned-artifact test unlinked a file that the next sub-case
  re-read. Repaired with a sentinel and a restore. The first mutation harness tree lacked the
  intent-qualification fixture the planning fixture reads (builder procedure, no product
  effect); the archive list now includes it.
- **F3 (exposed-by this slice; carried for a decision).** The create template is the F-04.2
  disposable fixture: a bare agent with no tools, no initial message, `autotitle` false and the
  tag `{"aieh": "f-04.2-disposable"}`. Admission sends exactly the template, so every admitted
  body is that conversation until the profile pins another template. A real attempt needs its
  agent definition (from the catalog pin), its initial message and its own tags; whether those
  come from a per-stage template pinned in the profile or from a catalog-derived block that
  admission validates is a design question for the leaf that composes the attempt's agent
  (A-01.x, E-01.3.x). Not decided here. Today every dispatch is refused anyway (no operational
  profile, no approved catalog pin).
- **F4 (introduced-by this slice, by design point 5; declared).** `POLICY_FORMAT` is now v2. The
  pinned golden policy digest of the E-01.2.1 fixture changed from `45eeaeaf...e90b` (v1) to
  `10ba1b157d89011ec93696be0dd8190697bc06244cfbeaeeb0623a984fb8a9d4` (v2). The E-01.2.1 tests
  were edited only where v2 requires: the synthetic profile declaration and its two artifacts,
  the expected policy inputs, the golden digest, and five admission variants in the per-input
  digest test. Every E-01.2.1 refusal test is unchanged and passes. A profile without an
  admission declaration is now refused at resolution (`PROFILE_ADMISSION_INVALID`), after the
  existing profile checks and before any Work read.
- **F5 (exposed-by E-01.2.1, closed here).** The E-01.2.1 identifier grammar and the manifest
  checker admit Windows device names (`con`, `nul`, `com1`, ...) as repository ids; the
  resolver resolves them. Admission refuses their workspace (`WORKSPACE_RESERVED_NAME`, through
  the real resolver). Refusing them earlier, in the manifest checker, would be a schema change;
  not done.
- **F6 (exposed-by; upstream; carried).** Two recorded deviations cannot be closed at the
  create: `host.docker.internal:host-gateway` and no `--network none` (`registry.py` adds and
  omits them unconditionally; F-04.2 section 7). No create field controls them. The third,
  `request-caps-override`, is closed on the dispatch side: no admitted body carries a cap other
  than the declared one, and the declared caps times the concurrent runs are within the
  ceilings.
- **F7 (unrelated-but-detected; not changed, out of bounds).** The `integrations/` layout row
  and the Quick start sentence of `docs/development.md`, and `AGENTS.md`, still describe the
  dispatch package as input resolution only.
- **Path boundary:** reads stayed in the checkout and the default TEMP; the managed root,
  `D:\aieh-guest-state`, the Credential Manager and WSL were not touched.
