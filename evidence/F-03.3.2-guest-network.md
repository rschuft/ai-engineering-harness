## 1. What was built

**A validation mode, `tools/guest.py netprobe` (test only).** Ordinary operation cannot
use it.
- **Host side.** One child process, `guest.py netprobe-listen`, binds three sockets on
  127.0.0.1 at OS-chosen ports:
  - the **test listener**, the only guestfwd target. It answers one line protocol:
    `AIEH-NETPROBE HELLO <label>` gets a fresh `NONCE <32 hex>`, and
    `ECHO <nonce>` gets `OK <label>` when it matches;
  - a **TCP decoy** that sends `AIEH-DECOY` and reads at most 256 bytes;
  - a **UDP decoy** that only logs.

  Every connection, line and datagram is logged as JSON lines with its peer and byte
  count. The process serves until its stdin reaches EOF, which is the harness's stop
  signal. The harness checks the bindings with `Get-NetTCPConnection` and
  `Get-NetUDPEndpoint` before the guest starts (127.0.0.1 only, or the cycle is
  refused), self-checks both decoys before and after, and checks after the stop that
  the process exited and left no socket.
- **Guest side.** The seed's user-data becomes cloud-init multipart: part 1 is the
  ordinary rendered user-data, byte for byte; part 2 is the pinned probe
  `profiles/strong-windows-qemu-whpx/validation/netprobe/netprobe.py` (sha256
  `76e30302…eb5ba1`, pinned in `guest.py` and in `profile.json`), with its one
  parameter line rendered (validated literal addresses and ports only). cloud-init runs
  it once as root. It writes `/var/lib/aieh-netprobe/result.json` (0644), which the host
  reads as `agent`.
- **QEMU.** The rendered profile argv gets one change: `,guestfwd=tcp:10.0.2.100:7000-
  tcp:127.0.0.1:<listener port>` appended to the user `-netdev`.
- **Records.** `launch.json` carries a `validation` block: mode `netprobe`, single boot,
  the profile argv's digest, the argv change, the guestfwd rule and chardev label, the
  listener's pid and ports, the script and rendered digests, the seed part digests and
  the parameters, beside the full effective `argv` (Owner constraint 7).
- **Guards.** `create` takes no overlay (only `netprobe` passes one, and the CLI's
  `create` has no such option); `launch` refuses any guestfwd that no validation record
  declares; `start` refuses a validation instance.
- **Machine verdict.** `netprobe` and `inspect` give profiles P7 for a validation
  instance from the guest's routes, counters and probe results and the host's listener
  log, self-checks, bindings and chardev connection owner. For an ordinary instance,
  P7 keeps its unprivileged probes, checks that the argv has no guestfwd, and cites the
  enforcement point's status.

**Why the overlay lives under `profiles/<profile>/validation/`.** The probe is specific
to this profile's network (SLIRP's 10.0.2.0/24, its gateway MAC, its IPv6 defaults).
`create` records the profile revision and refuses uncommitted changes under
`profiles/`, so every validation guest names the committed probe it ran. The directory
name and the file's header mark it test only; no ordinary code path reads it.

**`tools/check_profile.py`** now validates a structured enforcement point: a status of
`candidate`, `qualified` or `failed`; a qualified or failed one cites existing evidence
and its leaf; a qualified one has a reachability table with permitted and denied rows;
a pinned overlay matches its digest; a deferred endpoint has no port; and
`launch.argv` has exactly as many guestfwd rules as active guestfwd endpoints (zero
today).

## 10. Limits and carried items

- **Container approximation.** A network namespace with a veth pair and guest
  MASQUERADE is Docker's default-bridge path, but not Docker: no Docker-managed
  iptables chains, no `host.docker.internal:host-gateway` entry, no user-defined
  networks, and IPv6 untested in the namespace. **Carried to S-02.x and E-01** (a real
  container inside the guest, once Moby is provisioned under F-04.1).
- **The deferred endpoints.** `inference` and `provision` will use the qualified
  mechanism (a guestfwd to a 127.0.0.1 broker or proxy). Each needs separate Owner
  authorization to enter `launch.argv`; `check_profile.py` FAILs a guestfwd without an
  active endpoint. A guestfwd chardev is opened once at QEMU start, so its broker must
  run before the guest and for its whole life.
- **SLIRP's ICMP echo for `10.0.2.2` and `10.0.2.3`** answers from inside QEMU; no host
  socket is involved.
- **Probe duration.** A validation guest takes about 90 s to become ready, most of it
  the two 40 s resolver timeouts.
- **Line endings.** The pinned probe, like the pinned seed templates, is hashed as
  committed (LF). `.gitattributes` does not pin these paths, so a checkout with
  `core.autocrlf=true` that rewrites them would fail the digest checks (refusal, not a
  silent pass). Recorded, not changed here.
