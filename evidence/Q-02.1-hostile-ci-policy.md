### 2.3 Where the design left room, and how it was resolved locally

- **Decision 0016 is not committed** (the Overseer commits it). Both JSON files name it as
  `{id: "0016", record: null, note}`; the checker fails with `DECISION_UNLINKED` as soon as a
  `decisions/0016-*.md` exists that they do not link.
- **The live check** is the administration command's (revision 2, section 6.6), so the checker
  has no `--gitea` mode; `--live-report FILE` exists as an interface and exits 2 without reading
  anything (rulings R5, R6).
- **YAML with the standard library:** the checker reads a strict subset (block mappings and
  sequences, plain and quoted scalars, `{}`, `[]`, literal blocks, full-line comments) and fails
  closed on anything else.
- **Interpolation** is enforced more strictly than section 7.3 states it: an expression is
  admitted only as one of the job's three input variables; no `run:` text holds one. The first
  step validates the variables against the declared patterns and the allowlist.
- **Step keys** that could skip or soften a validation step (`if:`, `continue-on-error:`) are
  rejected, as part of "the first steps absent".
- **The repository allowlist is empty**, so every validation fails closed until a leaf adds a
  repository (the fixture at Q-02.2.2).
- **Where a profile variant declares its `config.yaml`:** `provisioning.ci_runner.config` (a
  JSON mirror), named in the profile for Q-02.2.1. The user id, the limits and the job-side
  mount path (`/aieh/exchange`) are proposals with status `planned`; the option names are
  decided.
- **`cred-runner-registration`'s custody** follows section 6.3: holder and manager
  `gitea-site-admin`, readable in transit by the dispatcher and the runner service, rotation
  owner `gitea-site-admin`; it maps to the trusted lane, so the runner host holds only its runner
  token. `cred-runner-token` is `scoped`, issued by Gitea (`external:gitea-actions`).
- **The trusted lane's domains** are `td-owner`, `td-management`, `td-privileged-effect` and
  `td-git-hosting` (the last for the site administrator's custody of the registration token);
  section 1.2 names the first three.
- **The new role's realization:** `tools/tests/test_check_role_tokens.py` pins every
  realization row's `verify_at`, and this leaf may not edit it, so `ci-lane-coordinator` carries
  its planned realization in `notes` (realization rows: none), the verifier's planned read is in
  its `target`, and the reworded `ci-runner-service` row keeps its prefix "The runner's
  registration secret".
- **`security/README.md`** mirrors the inventory edits: its checker requires every operation and
  finding id to be named there.
