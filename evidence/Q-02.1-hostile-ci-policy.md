**Required-workflow feature at the pin: yes, "scoped workflows"**: a source repository
registered for an owner or the instance, with per-workflow `Required` and status patterns
(`models/actions/scoped_workflow.go:18-38`); files under `SCOPED_WORKFLOW_DIRS` (default
`.gitea/scoped_workflows`); the content from the source repository's default branch, the run
created in the consuming repository with its token and secrets
(`notifier_helper.go:375-391,684-790`); only where the consuming repository's Actions unit is
enabled (`notifier_helper.go:146`).

Architect's revision 2 rests on the corrected facts and adds G10 (scoped workflows); the
Overseer resolved the stop and directed the build against it.
